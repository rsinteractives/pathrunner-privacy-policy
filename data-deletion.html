PathRunner - Data Deletion Request

Developer: RS Interactives

App: PathRunner

Request Data Deletion

We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. If you would like to request deletion of your data collected by PathRunner, please follow the steps below.

Step 1: Send an email to our support team
📧 Send Deletion Request Email
Step 2: Include the following information in your email:
  • Subject: "PathRunner Data Deletion Request"
  • Your device information (optional but helpful)
  • Confirmation that you want your data deleted
Step 3: We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you request data deletion, the following data will be removed or anonymized:

  • Analytics Data: App usage statistics, gameplay events (Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash Reports: Error logs and diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Advertising Data: Ad interaction data and device identifiers (AdMob)
  • Purchase History: In-app purchase records will be anonymized (RevenueCat)

What Data Will Be Kept?

Some data may be retained for legal or business purposes:

  • Transaction Records: Purchase receipts may be kept for tax and legal compliance (anonymized)
  • Aggregated Analytics: Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you

Retention Period: Any retained data will be kept for a maximum of 7 years for legal compliance, then permanently deleted.

Alternative: Uninstall the App

If you simply want to stop using PathRunner and remove your local game data:

  • Uninstall the app from your device - this will delete all local game progress
  • Analytics and advertising data will automatically expire after 14 months
⚠️ Important: Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your game progress, premium purchase status, and all associated data will be lost.

Processing Timeline

  • Acknowledgment: Within 3 business days
  • Deletion Completion: Within 30 days
  • Confirmation Email: Sent when deletion is complete
